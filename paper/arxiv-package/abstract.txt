We preregister and run a replication of TreeOPSD (Tree-Aware Posterior Distillation for Long-Horizon Agentic Reinforcement Learning, arXiv:2610.23181) on Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 with search-agent training. Training was on HotpotQA. Tier 2 therefore measures transfer to BrowseComp-Plus (BC+, 830 questions), per PREREG Amendment 1, where Zou et al. train their deep-research models on OpenResearcher. Against the paper's own control, unclipped TreePO, the pooled question-bootstrap exact-match gaps are +3.76 [+2.81, +4.76] on the four-set multi-hop QA average (tier 1) and +3.45 [+2.17, +4.78] on BC+ (tier 2). The result passes the F1 EM bars on those question-bootstrap intervals. That is not an F1 mechanism pass: the preregistered M1 inactivity rule also fired at the paper's dose (below), under which the posterior term is declared inactive whatever the EM shows. The per-seed intervals include zero (seed-level t intervals at n=3: [-4.06, +11.57] tier 1, [-6.13, +13.03] tier 2), and the largest contributor is one control seed that began to destabilise at the paper's dose. Against an added control, TreePO with ordinary ratio clipping, the BC+ gap is +0.08 [-1.24, +1.41] and the QA gap is +1.98 [+1.03, +2.94] pooled but declining across seeds (3.78, 2.10, 0.05). The posterior term changes the per-update target by a median 1.05% at the paper's dose, below the preregistered 2% M1 inactivity line. In the preregistered dose sweep the only reported accuracy point before collapse is one E=8 subset gap of -0.8 EM with no interval; at E=16, where drift reached 4.79% median and 7.74% in the last quarter, the unclipped control had already failed so there is no accuracy gap to plot. At 4x dose the unclipped control failed in training on both seeds (interim subset scores 0.0/0.0 and 17.8/0.0), while the clipped TreePO and TreeOPSD runs we report completed. The budget match claimed for tree sampling holds in generated tokens (1.03x GRPO) but not in trained tokens (1.48x), in an implementation that does not use Zou et al.'s unique-turn reconstruction. Best-checkpoint selection on our subset grid shrinks the gap toward zero. Zou et al. distinguish ratio clipping, which truncates an update without defining a target distribution, from their finite posterior target. Against that distinction, our BC+ result says the finite-target loss did not beat ordinary clipping of the same tree backup in this setting. It does not say any bound works: in Zou et al.'s Table 1 the bounded-ratio GSPO baseline scores 43.15 MHQA and 9.88 BC+ on Qwen3-4B, against GRPO 43.37 and 10.00 and TreeOPSD 44.13 and 13.98. The boundedness finding supported here is narrower: adding ordinary clipping to the same tree backup removed the instability we saw in the unclipped control and matched TreeOPSD on BC+. Clipped TreePO's three-seed QA-average sd was 0.80 EM; its BC+ sd was 2.09 EM.
